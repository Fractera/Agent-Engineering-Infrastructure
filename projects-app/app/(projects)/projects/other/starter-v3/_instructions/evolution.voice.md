# EVOLUTION `voice` — how the automation speaks

> **Law of instructions: ENGLISH, COMPACT, one fact one home.** Its reader is a model that must read the core too, so every line here is paid on every session. Prune before you append; never restate what another instruction already says — link to it by name.

Writes ONE field: `assistant` tab → `data.voice` — `{emoji, length, address}`. Content is never touched.

**Structure, not prose.** A structured preference is applied deterministically by the speech layer, cannot
be "read differently", and does not grow over time. Prose preferences belong to `behavior`.

**A direct request is applied AT ONCE.** The accumulation threshold (П2) exists for weak signals inferred by
observation — not for what a person said in plain words. Someone who has to repeat himself rightly considers
the automation deaf.

**Silence preserves.** A request about emoji does not clear an earlier request about length: only the field
actually mentioned is written.

**In the reply it outranks the instruction** — the instruction was written once by the owner, this was asked
by the person just now, and it must survive every run, channel and restart.
