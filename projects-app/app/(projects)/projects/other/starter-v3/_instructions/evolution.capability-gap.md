see it. That is why it is the safest area to prove, and it pays off immediately: the owner learns what people
keep asking for.

stops being a dead end with a notice: the gap is written down, and the owner decides. Vector 2 of this step
carries the chain further: a clarifying dialogue, a use case, and a launched development.

**No improvisation.** The node never tries to serve the impossible "approximately" and never promises it for
later. An invented capability here is the same critical breach as a no-op stub.

Writes to the **run journal** (`capability-gap` table), not to an entity store: it records WHAT HAPPENED —
we were asked for something absent — not an entity of the subject area.
