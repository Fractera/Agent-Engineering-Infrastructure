# CLASS `small-talk` — politeness: an answer is owed, an action is not

> **Law of instructions: ENGLISH, COMPACT, one fact one home.** Its reader is a model that must read the core too, so every line here is paid on every session. Prune before you append; never restate what another instruction already says — link to it by name.

**Where the answer lives:** in courtesy. **Routes to:** an output, touching nothing.

«Hello», «thanks», «got it». No store is involved, no tool, no record.

## Why it deserves a class of its own

Without it, «thanks» falls into the widest content class and becomes a record — and the owner watches the
automation solemnly file his politeness. Empty records are not harmless: they pollute exactly the store the
owner will later search, and they make every count wrong.

## How it recognizes

Deterministically, from short greeting and acknowledgement forms anchored to the START of the message: a
greeting is an opening, and «hello, record this» is not small talk. Narrow on purpose.

## What it must never do

- **Never write anything.** Not a record, not a log entry that pretends to be one.
- **Never take over the conversation.** One short answer that points at what the automation is for. It is a
  door-opener, not a personality.
- **Never claim a message that also carries a task.**

## Reuse

Domain-neutral and worth revealing everywhere: it is the cheapest way to stop an automation from looking
absurd on the first message.
