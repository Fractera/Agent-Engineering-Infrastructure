The last step: make sure the agent can work with a **real server**, not only with your
machine.

**The difference between the two addresses.** `localhost:3000` is the project on your
computer: only you see it, and only while it runs. Your domain or IP is the project **on
the internet**: visitors see it, and it works while your computer is off. The first is for
development; the second is your actual site.

Copy the instruction below. The first deployment takes longer than usual: the agent is
learning how your application is put together. Errors during it are a normal part of the
work, and it will record them so they are not repeated.

Tick the box once you have seen your company name **at your own address on the internet**,
not on `localhost:3000`.
