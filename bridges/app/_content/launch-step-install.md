Ask the agent to install dependencies — the programs without which the starter template
will not run. Watch the installation: it takes a few minutes.

When it finishes, ask it to **run the project on `localhost:3000`**.

**What `localhost:3000` is.** It is your project's address **on your own machine**. Paste
it into a browser and you will see your application. It does not face the internet: it
lives only while the project runs on your computer.

**Port taken by another app?** Stop that app and run again. Two projects do not share one
port.

**Did not work?** Do not move on and do not fix it yourself — ask the agent: "The project
will not start on localhost:3000, find out why and fix it." That is what it is for.

Tick the box once you have seen your application in the browser.
