**Migration is not a third road from here — it is the continuation of the first one.**

If your project is written on another framework — WordPress, Laravel, plain React, Next without our
architecture — the button next door cannot connect its repository. It will not build, and the panel
will say so plainly. Moving it across is possible, and the order is this:

1. **Take the first option — the Fractera starter.** Install and run it: walk the whole wizard to the
   end, until your project exists and you can see it at your own address.
2. **Open the “Development mode” tab** and choose the **“Migration”** mode.
3. **Read that mode's instructions.** It opens with four questions — what kind of application you
   have, whether there will be sign-in, whether access will be role-based, and which roles those are.
   The answers settle the whole layout rather than a single door, which is why they are asked before
   the first page and the first table.
4. **Name the source** — the address of your current project's repository, or a folder on your machine.

From there a dedicated procedure, its skills and its tools carry your project through the change into
the Fractera architecture. At the end you have what everyone else has: an application you can develop
and scale safely.

> **Why the starter comes first.** Migration takes a foreign project apart and builds it again on our
> architecture. There is nothing to build into while the architecture is not on the server: the
> working starter first, the move into it second.
