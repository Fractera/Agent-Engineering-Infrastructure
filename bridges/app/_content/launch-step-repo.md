Your project lives on this server. GitHub is the only road by which it can leave —
to your own machine, to another server, to a colleague. Without it the work exists
in exactly one place, and that place is a rented machine.

Create an empty repository, then paste its address below and press **Check
repository**. The panel will try to reach it and will say plainly what went wrong
if it cannot.

**Private or public is your choice.** Private is the safe default: the repository
will hold your configuration files.
