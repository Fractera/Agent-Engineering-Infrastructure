The project is connected and built. Now look at it **with your own eyes** and decide
whether it works as a starting point.

Open your address on the internet. It is enough that the project is **in working order** —
pages open, nothing crashes. Everything else you will edit during development; that is
what the agent is for.

**All good?** Tick the box and move on — the normal launch route lies ahead.

**Something wrong?** Two paths below. You can **restore the Fractera starter template** and
run the launch again from a clean slate. Or you can **write to us** — we carry projects
over to the Fractera architecture ourselves.

> We did not check in advance that the connected repository is built on the Fractera
> architecture — and if that is the problem, you need the migration process. It lets you
> use the starter template and, with a dedicated algorithm, skills and tools, transform
> your project so development can continue.
