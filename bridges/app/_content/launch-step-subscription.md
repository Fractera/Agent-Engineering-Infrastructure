Activate your subscription. Claude Code will open a browser and ask you to sign in.

**This is where almost every newcomer trips.** A subscription is tied to the browser you
paid in. Claude Code opens your **last active** browser — and if that is a different one,
the subscription will not be found there, and the reason stays invisible. Make sure the
active browser is the one the subscription was bought in.

> ### 💡 Tip: two $20 subscriptions instead of one at $100
>
> The model reaches full strength after roughly **300,000 tokens** and holds it until
> **800,000**. One $20 subscription, worked without pause, lasts about **400,000 tokens** —
> some three hours on Opus in high mode. In other words it runs out exactly in the most
> effective middle of the conversation.
>
> The cheaper answer is not the $100 tier: take **two or three $20 subscriptions** on
> separate Gmail accounts, each in its own browser. Hit the limit — open the next browser,
> return to Claude Code, repeat the activation. It will go through in the new browser,
> where the limit is untouched, and work continues **in the same conversation**, losing no
> context.
>
> We recommend keeping at least two: enough to run the whole million-token window in one
> stretch of development.
